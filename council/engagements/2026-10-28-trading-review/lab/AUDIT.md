# Pre-execution audit
Only the four authorized source files were read; none executed.

- Lookahead/execution: both engines observe a completed daily close, volume and indicators then fill decisions at that same close. Breakout ranking, targets, core trend and exits therefore assume an unavailable exact fill. Prior-window breakout highs are correctly lagged; resting stops are checked before current-day stop updates (good). A defensible variant needs next-open execution with prior completed signals, explicit stop activation and gaps.
- Returns: backtest.stats excludes initial purchase costs by normalizing to first post-trade equity. On deposit days it subtracts the gross deposit even though immediate core-buy fees reduce equity; its flow timing is neither consistently beginning nor end of period. Reported CAGR is time-weighted, not money-weighted. Deposits distort the raw-equity circuit breaker. Terminal holdings are marked without exit fees; closed-trade statistics omit open holdings. Rotation similarly omits initial fees from CAGR and has no deposits at all.
- Controls: built-in comparator is 100% BTC, not identical core plus cash. Cash/no-trading requires both --setups disabled and --no-cash-limit; otherwise fallback buys persist. Rotation trend includes weekly decisions, 25% stops and breaker: NOT a core-only 200-day rule. Its combo reports zero costs/trades despite constituent trading. Same core, deposits, dates, costs and liquidation convention are required.
- Rule mismatch: defaults are 20-day breakout, pullbacks, 2% risk and forced cash deployment versus brief's 55-day breakout-only, 4% risk. SPEC is only NEAR/AAVE/UNI; caps .10/.25/.50 do not cap all non-BTC risk. With 50% core, they are fractions of trading sleeve, not whole account. Current 68.5% BTC/ETH core differs from a 50% BTC example.
- Date/data: absent start silently maps to earliest candle, absent end silently maps to latest (including future requested end). BTC union calendar can hide missing days; missing held-asset closes undervalue positions or crash arithmetic. No candle validation, gap checking, delisting handling or point-in-time universe. Fetcher includes current incomplete UTC day, accepts rows outside requested windows, suppresses failures into partial/empty files and overwrites data. Default June 2022 history supplies enough warmup for January 2023's 200-day average, but not 365-day resistance; different target windows become available at different dates. New listings lack warmup and fixed surviving universe biases results.
- Costs: quantity is budget*(1-S)/price, a haircut convention rather than budget/(price*(1+S)); sell haircut is applied separately. At .0095 each side, flat-price round trip loses 1-(.9905)^2 = 1.890975%. No taxes, funding/cash yield, market impact or agent running costs modeled.

Decision: do not run supplied engines unmodified or promote their historical summary to verified results. Probe public data once before deciding whether an honest isolated reimplementation is feasible.
