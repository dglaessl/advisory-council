## Frame
Goal: an agent-driven, scalable business reaching ≥ $10k/month, run in ≤ 1 h per weekday plus weekends, with DACH as a priority market. It must not conflict with Daniel's agreement with The American Housing Corporation, a US homebuilder of rowhomes and a prefab system ([Fast Company](https://fastcompany.com/91484328/american-housing-corporation-rowhouse-solve-the-housing-crisis)). That means outside the employer's field and no employer time, devices, information or contacts.
**The constraint that decides this is time.** About 10 h/week rules out anything that runs on live services. $10k/month at about 10 h/week means about $250/h of effective yield, which only a product or a productized service can reach.

**Excluded from scoring:** idea 5, AI-supported mid-density development with Nate. It is the employer's field, so it moves to the post-employment real estate track (planning only).

## Options
**Daniel's ideas**
- **D1 Host agent for owner-hosts.** Marketing strategy, website, social media and a property-manager interface, built from the Casa La Fonte stack.
- **D2 Ready-to-use multi-agent council (Witmoot / Sagemoot / cnclv).** A general product for individuals, researchers and companies.
- **D3 Longevity/health knowledge products.** Courses, guides or a protocol tracker.
- **D4 The crypto trading agent offered to others.**

**New options (Strategist)**
- **N1 "KI-Kompetenz" programme (DACH).** A German-language AI-literacy course plus agent starter kits for professional firms and freelancers (lawyers, tax advisers, medical practices, engineering offices outside construction and housing). Demand driver: since 2 Feb 2025, EU AI Act Art. 4 requires providers and deployers to ensure staff AI literacy ([Austrian regulator RTR](https://rtr.at/rtr/service/ki-servicestelle/ai-act/ai_literacy.en.html); [Covington](https://www.insideprivacy.com/artificial-intelligence/european-commission-provides-guidance-on-ai-literacy-requirement-under-the-eu-ai-act/)).
- **N2 "Buy-and-host" due-diligence reports for DACH buyers of Italian rural homes.** Agents assemble the land-registry (cadastral) and planning-compliance checklist, rental comps, a renovation-cost range and yield. Daniel signs off as architect and host. This is advice, not Italian design services. It leads naturally into D1. Evidence: Germans made 8.8% of Gate-away's 2025 foreign purchases and Tuscany was the top region at 14.8%; foreign buyers make up about 60% of sales in central Italy ([The Local, Feb 2026](https://www.thelocal.it/20260225/explained-who-owns-all-the-second-homes-in-italy)).
- **N3 Evidence-review council (D2 aimed at one vertical).** Agentic reviews of the research literature with evidence grading and recorded dissent, sold as briefs to longevity clinics, health-tech founders, investors and science journalists. It reuses the planned Scientific Researcher and Longevity Scientist seats. It is a sharper first market for D2 and a natural home for the "Enlightenment" framework: transparent reasoning, open dissent, *sapere aude*.
- **N4 Fractional AI product leadership.** Advisory retainers for DACH firms outside housing. This is the baseline for comparison: the fastest cash, but not agent-driven.

**Evidence notes**
- **D1.** About 455k of Germany's 555k holiday homes are privately rented ([Deutscher Ferienhausverband/Statista via Tageskarte, 2024 study, 2022 data](https://www.tageskarte.io/tourismus/detail/deutscher-ferienhausmarkt-generiert-29-milliarden-euro-umsatz-pro-jahr.html)). Reported AI use among small hosts went from 14% to 58% ([PriceLabs via Short Term Rentalz](https://shorttermrentalz.com/tag/global-host-report/); publication date unclear, verify). Incumbents such as Hostaway, Hospitable and Lodgify are adding AI ([Hostaway](https://www.hostaway.com/blog/core-tools-for-short-term-rental-hosts/); [Lodgify via Short Term Rentalz](https://shorttermrentalz.com/tag/lodgify/)). The software layer is therefore a commodity; the winnable part is **direct-booking marketing in the owner's language**.
- **D2.** Horizontal agent platforms are crowded. "Conclaves AI" already exists (the Research Analyst is checking).
- **D4.** Managing others' crypto with an automated agent is likely licensable portfolio management under BaFin rules ([BaFin warning example](https://www.btc-echo.de/news/bafin-keine-zulassung-fuer-bitcoin-trade-robot-66792/); [fin-law.de on signal providers](https://fin-law.de/en/2020/06/22/trading-signals-for-crypto-assets-do-signal-providers-have-to-register-with-bafin/)), and the agenda puts its current edge at about $70 a year.
- **Architecture-practice AI** (considered, not proposed). UK practices using AI on most projects went from 59% in 2025 to 74% in 2026 ([RIBA 2025](https://www.riba.org/news/second-riba-ai-report-shows-surge-in-usage-among-uk-architects/); [RIBA 2026](https://www.riba.org/news/ai-adoption-reaches-tipping-point-across-uk-architecture/)). That is strong demand, but it sits too close to a VP of Product at a building-system company, so I treat it as a conflict for now.

## Recommended shortlist and November tests
All tests are validation only. No sales, entity or paid launch before the agreement check (week 1, below).

**A. Host agent narrowed to DACH owners of holiday homes in Italy and the Alps, with N2 as the paid entry product.**
- Why: proven on real guests, an unusual mix (architect, host, native German), and a clear chain from buying a house to its first booking.
- Test (3 weeks):
  - 12 German-language interviews with owners from Facebook owner groups and the directories Casa La Fonte already uses.
  - A German landing page with two offers, "Direktbuchungs-Check" and "Kauf-Check Italien", collecting waitlist sign-ups only.
  - One free sample report on a public listing.
- Pass: ≥ 5 owners say they would pay ≥ €150/month or ≥ €1.5k per report, and ≥ 30 waitlist sign-ups.

**B. Evidence-review council (D2 through the N3 vertical).**
- Why: maximum agent leverage, the strongest portfolio piece, low conflict risk, and it tests the "Witmoot/Sagemoot" thesis on one paying vertical before building a general platform.
- Test (4 weeks):
  - Produce 3 evidence briefs on public longevity questions, with the search protocol, evidence grades and recorded dissent. Use no personal Ledger data.
  - Measure Daniel's minutes per brief.
  - Show the briefs to 10 target buyers (clinic founders, health-tech investors, writers).
- Pass: ≥ 3 ask for a paid pilot at ≥ €500 per brief, and ≤ 60 min of Daniel's time per brief.
- Hold off on domains: buy only the .com (~$13) until the vertical is confirmed.

**C. KI-Kompetenz DACH (N1).**
- Why: there is a compliance driver, German is his first language, it runs on weekends, and it builds the AI-expert road map at the same time.
- Test (3 weeks):
  - Outline a 4-module course on Art. 4 plus a starter agent.
  - 10 conversations with practice owners outside construction and housing.
  - A waitlist page.
- Pass: ≥ 3 firms willing to pay ≥ €2k for an in-house session, or ≥ 25 waitlist sign-ups.

**Drop or park:**
- D4: keep it as a public write-up only.
- D3: fold into B; no consumer health product for now.
- N4: incompatible with both the time limit and the conflict boundary.

## Conflict check: flagged (needs review before any test that involves outside contact)
1. **Invention assignment.** The council and possibly the Casa La Fonte stack were built after he joined (May 2026). Texas, unlike California (Labor Code §2870), has no statute limiting what an invention-assignment clause can claim. Check four things:
   - which state's law governs the agreement;
   - how broadly it defines "related to the Company's business or anticipated research", which matters because the employer may use AI agents in product development;
   - the clauses on outside activity and moonlighting;
   - the rules on public writing.
2. **Field.** Keep away from housing, homebuilding, prefab, construction tech and architecture-practice tooling. A and N2 are residential real estate in Italy, not US homebuilding, but "real estate" may be defined broadly in the agreement, so they need review.
3. **Practical.** No employer devices, time or contacts. Validation outreach goes only to people met outside work.
4. **Confidentiality.** This draft uses no employer material.
If the terms are unclear, the cleanest path is a written carve-out from the employer for named projects. That is Daniel's decision; it involves contacting his employer.
