The best infill sites are often the hardest to build
Low-rise infill is the missing middle of housing, too big for small builders and too small for large
developers. Yet walkable, mid-density streets with shops and cafés, like those across Europe, are
often the most sought-after parts of a city. We designed our system to build these buildings
efficiently, cost-effectively and repeatably.

APPENDIX · UNDERWRITING



How we underwrite a site
Every site gets the same ground-up model and is ranked on one number:
unlevered yield on cost.

01    Screen — public parcel and zoning data find lots that need only a building permit

02    Fit — the 12′ × 12′ grid is laid on the buildable footprint and stacked to the height limit,
      giving homes, area and parking

03    Price — rents are set from local area median income, less operating costs and vacancy

04    Cost — costs are built line by line on 517 W Main’s budget codes

05    Rank — sites that clear the hurdle move forward, and the fund selects three
